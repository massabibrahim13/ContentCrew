How a post gets made

Three agents share the work. Twice along the way, everything stops until you decide.

  1. 1

    Plan

    The Supervisor reads your request and company context, then lays out the steps.

    Supervisor Agent
  2. 2

    Research

    Searches competitor content, reads the articles, and maps keywords and gaps.

    Analysis Agent
  3. 3

    Approve the strategy

    Check the sources, keywords and outline. Approve it, or say what to change.

    You
  4. 4

    Write

    Turns the approved strategy into an original draft, not a copy of competitors.

    Generation Agent
  5. 5

    Review the draft

    Edit it yourself, ask for a rewrite, or approve it for publishing.

    You
  6. 6

    Publish

    Only after your approval does the post go out through the blog API.

    Supervisor Agent
